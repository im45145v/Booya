DemoLens 0.6.0 adds camera layouts. You can show the screen alone, the screen with a camera bubble, the camera next to the screen, or the camera full screen, and change between them during the video. Tall and square videos now follow the cursor. Camera recording now works on Windows too.

## Layouts
- **Four layouts**: screen only, camera bubble, side by side and camera full screen. Pick the one for the whole recording under Style > Layout.
- **Change during the video**: the timeline has a new LAYOUT lane when the recording has a camera. Double click to add a block, drag it, drag its edges, choose its layout, delete it. Blocks never overlap, and a short **Blend** glides from one layout to the next. The editor preview and the export use the same renderer, so what you see is what you get.
- **Vertical and square**: in 9:16 and 1:1 the screen shows a window that follows the cursor and blends towards the zoom, and the camera sits above or below it. Turn this off to keep the whole screen with bars.
- Projects without a camera look and behave as before. Old projects open unchanged.

## Camera on Windows
- Webcam recording uses DirectShow. It picks the first real camera and skips virtual ones (OBS, Snap and similar) unless you name one with `DEMOLENS_CAMERA`.

## Other
- The timeline no longer failed to draw its lane labels in recordings without a camera (found by the new layout tests).
- `docs/COMPETITORS.md` now compares DemoLens with ScreenCharm feature by feature. The gaps still open are device frames, motion blur, background music and muting a single clip.

## Honest limits
- The Windows camera code and the editor app shell were verified by CI compile only. No Windows computer with a camera was available.
- Layouts are verified with the real renderer and exporter on synthetic recordings in 4 layouts and 3 shapes, and with the editor in Chrome. They have not been run in the macOS web view.
- Face tracking and camera cutout are not part of this release.
- System audio is still Linux only. Everything listed under "Honest limits" in 0.5.2 still applies.
