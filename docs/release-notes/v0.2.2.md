DemoLens 0.2.2 fixes the problems found while testing 0.2.1 on a MacBook Air (macOS 27, Apple Silicon): quiet sound, a harsh zoom, the "couldn't decode frame" error, shortcuts that did nothing, silent AI features, a sluggish editor and a pile of empty recording folders.

This release was built and checked on CI. It has not been run on a Mac by the author, so the sync and loudness fixes below are verified with synthetic tests only. Please run `demolens verify` on a new recording (see "Check it on your Mac").

## Quiet sound
- Exports and the editor preview now bring the voice to **-16 LUFS** (two pass loudness normalisation plus a limiter), so a microphone that records at -60 dB is audible. A silent track is left alone instead of being boosted into noise.
- The editor plays sound again. A small hidden audio player follows the playhead, clip boundaries and speed changes.
- **Microphone chooser** and a **live level meter** in the toolbar, before and during recording. The meter turns amber when the microphone is very quiet. The built-in microphone is the default, so a Bluetooth headset no longer drops the recording into the low quality call mode.
- Style tab, Audio: loudness on or off, boost (0 to 24 dB), volume, fades and a **sync nudge** (-1000 to +1000 ms).
- **A/V sync**: on macOS the microphone, screen and event log now share one wall clock start time and the audio is resampled to follow it, which removes the roughly 0.3 s lag seen in 0.2.1. `demolens verify` reports the measured offset.

## Recording
- A new **recording pill**: a pulsing red dot, the time, microphone level, Pause, Stop and Discard. Frosted on macOS, draggable, dims when idle, left out of the video. A **3-2-1 countdown** runs first; click it to cancel.
- Folders are only created when capture really starts. A failed start no longer leaves an empty `recording-*` folder behind.
- Recordings are remuxed to MP4 with the index at the front once you stop, so the length is always known.
- **Global shortcuts** (work in any app): Cmd+Shift+R starts or stops, Cmd+Shift+P pauses (Ctrl on Windows and Linux). If another app owns a key the editor says so.

## Zoom
- Slower and smoother: cubic ease in and out (about 1 s each way), the zoom starts easing before the click, holds at least 1.6 s, merges nearby clicks and pans between targets instead of zooming out and in. Default level 1.7x.
- Style tab, Zoom: **Speed** and **Level** sliders and "Apply to auto zoom".

## Editor
- **"couldn't decode frame at 5363ms" is gone.** Seeks are clamped to the last frame, the duration comes from the container (the old MKV files had none), and the last good frame is shown at the end.
- **Faster preview**: a 720p editing copy (hardware encoded when possible) is built in the background after recording or opening a video. The editor decodes that instead of the full 2940x1912 frames. Exports still use the original. See "Speed" below.
- Keyboard shortcuts work on macOS: a native menu (File, Edit, Record, Window, Help) with Cmd+Z, Shift+Cmd+Z, Cmd+S, Cmd+O, Cmd+E, Cmd+, and Cmd+/, plus Space/K, J/L, arrows, S, Z and Delete. A shortcut sheet (Help menu or `?`) lists them all, with Cmd on macOS.

## AI status
- The Magic tab has an **AI status** panel: every feature shows **Ready**, **Needs model** (with a Download button and its size), **Using cloud** or **Error**, plus a **Test** button that runs a tiny check on the installed model.
- **Download recommended models** fetches the speech and text recognition models in one go with a progress bar.
- After each run you get a clear result ("Transcribed 42 words", "Found 3 secrets and blurred 4 regions"). A transcript with no speech now says so instead of silently doing nothing.
- **Fixed: secret blurring never worked on a fresh install.** The text recognition models came from a host that now answers 403. DemoLens falls back to a pinned mirror and checks the SHA-256.
- `demolens doctor` now also lists each AI feature, the models folder and the microphones (`--self-test` runs the checks).

## Housekeeping
- Settings, **Disk usage**: how much space recordings use, a list that marks empty and broken folders, a clean-up button that asks first, and a button to clear the editing copies. Nothing is ever deleted without your confirmation.

## Settings
- **Cloud AI and keys are under the gear icon at the top right: Settings > Cloud & API keys** (renamed from "Cloud AI"). The AI status panel links straight to it. Upload settings are in Settings > Upload (S3).

## Speed
`demolens bench` makes a 2940x1912, 30 fps test recording and times decoding, the proxy and a 1080p export. CI runs it on a macOS runner and prints the numbers in the job summary. Numbers depend heavily on the machine and on how busy it is.

## Check it on your Mac
```bash
demolens doctor --self-test      # permissions, displays, mic list, AI readiness
demolens verify ~/Movies/DemoLens/recording-XXXX/recording.mp4   # A/V offset and click timing
demolens bench                   # speed on your machine
```
`demolens verify` prints `audio_offset_ms`. Anything within about 100 ms is fine; send the output if it is larger and use the Sync nudge meanwhile.

## Known limits
- Not notarised: run `xattr -cr /Applications/DemoLens.app` once. After an update macOS may ask again for Screen Recording and Microphone permission (remove DemoLens from the list, add it again, reopen).
- System audio on macOS still needs a loopback device such as BlackHole.
- Key capture on macOS (Input Monitoring) is not implemented yet, so the keystroke overlay has no data there.
- The editor preview is still rendered by DemoLens rather than played natively, so playback is lighter on 720p copies but not as smooth as a native video player. Sound is played natively.
