01Brand story
What Booya is

A screen recorder that
knows where to look.

Booya records your screen, writes captions on your own machine and zooms in when your clicks bunch up. It used to be called DemoLens. It is free, open source and built with Tauri, so it stays small and fast.

The brand should feel like the app: calm, quick, a little bit funny, and out of the way until you need it.

1Plain first

Ink on paper. One accent. If something needs a second colour, it probably needs fewer words instead.

2On purpose

Nothing moves, blinks or glows unless it tells you something. The ripple means a click. The orange means recording.

3Precise, not cold

Tight type, exact pixels, tidy spacing. The ghost is the one wink, and we only wink once.

4Yours

Offline by default. We say what stays on your machine and we never make it sound like magic.

02Meet Booya
The backstory

The forgotten arrow.

For years Booya was the cursor nobody looked at. It pointed, it clicked, it got cropped out of every demo. So it died a little, came back as a ghost, and now it haunts your cursor on purpose.

  • It zooms only when three clicks cluster. One click is a click. Three close together means you are showing something.
  • It blinks when recording starts and stops. Never in between. That is how you know it heard you.
  • It writes captions offline. It listens, it types, and nothing leaves your machine.

Poses only change the eyes, the ripple and one small prop. No arms, no mouths, no sparkles, no blush. Files: assets/mascot, light and dark, SVG and PNG.

04Logo anatomy
Construction

Built on the arrow.

  • Grid. The body is 1.00 wide and 1.32 tall, drawn on a 0.1 grid from the tip.
  • Edges. A straight stem on the left and a 45 degree edge from the tip to the shoulder.
  • Tail notch. At the shoulder line, the edge steps in to 0.60, then the tail runs down to the hem.
  • Hem. Three equal scallops, 0.26 wide, 0.077 deep.
  • Eyes. Two holes, diameter e = 0.156, centred 0.24 apart in the head. e is also the clear space unit.
  • Ripple. One quarter ring around the tip, radius 0.27, stroke 0.085, round caps. Always Signal, or mono.
  • Corners. A 0.035 round join softens the tip and shoulder without blunting them.

05Lockups, clear space and minimum sizes
Horizontal · primary

Mark body is 1.58 x the cap height. Gap is 0.42 x cap. The hem sits a hair under the baseline.

Stacked

For square spaces, splash screens and stickers. Body is 2.1 x cap.

Mark and wordmark

The wordmark is Geist SemiBold, tracked -3.5%, outlined. Never retype it.

Clear space

Keep at least one eye width e clear on every side, measured from the ripple, the stem and the hem. More is better.

Signal stays under 10% of any screen, and usually under 3%. It is the record dot, the Stop button, the ripple and the focus frame while recording.

Rules
  • 20 px grid, 2 px padding, drawn at 1.5 px stroke.
  • Round caps and joins, 2 px corner radius on rectangles.
  • One colour, currentColor. Filled shapes only for state, like the record dot.
  • Plain metaphors. A mic is a mic. The zoom icon borrows the focus frame.
  • Sizes: 16, 20 and 24. Scale the stroke with the size, never below 1.25.

14Captions and the focus frame
Click the gear, then pick Export settings.
Click the gear, then pick Export settings.

Caption overlay. Geist 500, 22/30, on Ink at 82%. Max two lines, 720 px wide, 26 px from the bottom. Same in light and dark, because it sits on the recording.

Focus frame. Four corners, 3 px, 26 px arms, 6 px outer radius, in Signal. It appears when three clicks cluster and eases in with the zoom.

Cursor. In recordings Booya can swap your pointer for the mark at 26 px, with a single ripple per click.

  • No ripple scale. Show a static ring for 200 ms, then fade.
  • No blink, no trail. Zoom cuts instead of easing.
  • Transitions drop to opacity only, 100 ms or less.

16Voice and tone
Tagline
Record it.
Booya.
  • Plain. Say what happened, then what to do.
  • Short. One idea per sentence. Most UI strings fit on one line.
  • Friendly, a little playful. The ghost joke lives in the mascot and the docs, not in buttons.
  • Never cutesy. No spooky puns in errors, no baby talk.
  • No hype. Skip revolutionary, magic, seamless, powerful, AI-powered.
  • No exclamation marks in the UI. Calm is the point.
