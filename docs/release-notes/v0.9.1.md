Booya 0.9.1 brings a new look for the whole app, keeps clicks and zoom in step with late starting recordings, and makes updates, deletes and exports safer around each other.

This is the first published build with the redesign. Version 0.9.0 was tagged but never published, because the Linux build could not download its packages from Ubuntu's mirror. Everything planned for 0.9.0 is in this release.

## New look
- The app has been redesigned around ink on paper (#51). With nothing open, the stage is a paper sheet with a "Show it once." headline, a short looping figure of the ghost cursor at work and a Recent ledger. Once a recording is open, the stage turns into a dark well for the video.
- Record is the one orange button. It shows a 3, 2, 1 countdown, then turns into a timer with a Stop segment while you record. The countdown can be turned off in Settings, under Appearance.
- The timeline has a timecode ruler, zoom brackets, a caption lane and a playhead with a timecode tab. It folds down to a slim ruler when nothing is open.
- The side panel uses numbered sections, switches and model cards that say whether each feature is ready, needs a model or needs a fix. Run auto edit explains why it is off when it can't run.
- The status bar shows the mic level in dB, the source, your models and the version.
- Prefer a dark stage? Settings, Appearance, Stage lets you pick Paper or Ink.

## Sync and recording
- Clicks and zoom stay lined up with the video even when the first frame arrives up to 30 seconds late. The old limit was 10 seconds (#48).
- When the mic and system audio start far apart, they now line up correctly in the mix, and the mic timing log is no longer deleted before its fix is applied (#45).
- Recording folders are named down to the millisecond, so two quick takes can't land in the same folder (#46).
- Restart recording asks to discard your changes only once. Throwing away a "Record here" take no longer makes the next recording get spliced in at the old spot (#50).

## Safety
- A new take or export can't start while an update is installing, and two lock calls that could crash the app no longer do (#43).
- Open, Recent, Sample and a new Record ask before they replace a project with unsaved changes (#44).
- Deleting a recording or your data waits for a running export to finish, and an update can't restart the app during an HTML export (#49).

## Behind the scenes
- CI jobs now have time limits, so a stuck test gets stopped instead of holding up everything after it (#47).
- Release and CI builds on Linux no longer hang when an Ubuntu mirror stops answering. Each package install has a time limit and moves on to another mirror if the first one fails (#53).

## Things to do
- On macOS, expect to grant Screen Recording again, because each build is a new ad hoc identity.

## Not tested on real hardware
The redesign was checked with screenshots and layout tests down to 1024 by 640. The sync changes in #45 and #48 were checked by CI and unit tests only. A clap test on a real Mac and Windows PC has not been done for this build.
