Open source screen recorder

Record it. Booya.

A screen recorder with a quiet ghost inside. It zooms in where you click, writes captions on your own computer, and keeps your voice, camera and screen in step. You do the demo. The ghost does the editing.

Download Booya View the source

Free and MIT licensed. For Apple Silicon Macs, Windows and Linux.

What the ghost does

The editing happens while you record.

Booya logs your clicks, typing and scrolls next to the video. The editor reads that log, so the boring parts of a demo video are done before you open it.

Auto zoom on clicks

Click a few times in one small area and the camera eases in. Start typing and it moves closer. Scroll and it drifts back out. Every zoom sits on the timeline, so you can drag it, resize it or delete it.

Captions on this device

Whisper runs locally through whisper.cpp. Captions are written word by word on your own machine, with the Wi-Fi off if you like. Your voice never leaves your laptop.

Webcam and layouts

Put your face in a circle, a corner, beside the screen or full frame, and switch layouts anywhere on the timeline. Voice, camera, captions and screen run on one clock.

Mac, Windows, Linux and touch

Apple Silicon Macs, Windows 10 and 11, and Linux on X11. On Windows and Linux a tap on a touch screen counts as a click, so the zoom follows your finger too. Touch is new and so far checked by automated tests, so real screen reports are welcome.

Secrets blurred for you

API keys, email addresses and card numbers are found and blurred before you share, and the blur follows the zoom. It helps a lot, but watch your export before you post it.

Export for anywhere

MP4, WebM or GIF up to 4K, in 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, with presets for YouTube, Reels, X and README GIFs. The preview is the export, pixel for pixel.

How it works

Three steps, and one of them is just talking.

  1. 01

    Record

    Pick a screen, a window or an area. A small pill keeps time and drops markers, and it never shows up in the video.

  2. 02

    Auto edit

    One button adds zooms and captions, cuts filler words and long pauses, blurs secrets and cleans up noise. One undo takes it all back.

  3. 03

    Export

    Pick a style and a destination. Booya renders the same frames you saw in the editor and saves them next to your recording.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your computer unless you say so.

Screen recordings are full of tokens, emails and customer data. Booya treats them that way. Read the full privacy notes.

  • No account and no telemetry. No analytics, no crash reports, no sign in.

  • Offline by default. The internet is used once, to download a caption model when you ask for it.

  • Your files stay yours. Recordings go to Videos/Booya in a folder only you can read.

  • Cloud is opt in. Bring your own key for a cloud provider or your own bucket. Booya shows what will be sent first.

  • Typing stays private. Key capture is off by default. When it is on, only shortcuts are kept, never the letters you type.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is Booya really free?

Yes. It is MIT licensed, with no paid tier, no watermark and no time limit. The whole thing is on GitHub.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes. Recording, editing, captions and export all run on your computer. You need a connection once to download a caption model, and again only if you turn on a cloud provider yourself.

macOS says Booya can't be opened. What do I do?

The Mac app is signed ad hoc rather than with a paid Apple Developer ID, so Gatekeeper stops it the first time. Any one of these works:

  1. In Applications, right click Booya, choose Open, then Open again.
  2. Or open it once, then go to System Settings, Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  3. If macOS says the app is damaged, run xattr -cr /Applications/Booya.app in Terminal and open it again. That only removes the quarantine flag your browser added.

Then allow Screen & System Audio Recording, and Microphone if you record your voice.

Windows shows "Windows protected your PC". Is that safe?

That is SmartScreen. It appears for new apps without a paid code signing certificate. Click More info, check that the file is the Booya installer you downloaded from GitHub, then click Run anyway.

Does it work on Intel Macs or Wayland?

Not out of the box yet. Release builds are for Apple Silicon, and Intel Macs can build from source. On Linux, log in with an X11 session for now. Wayland capture through PipeWire is on the roadmap.

Does Booya record what I type?

Key capture is off by default. When you turn it on, Booya keeps shortcuts such as Cmd+S and the timing of your typing, so it can zoom in on text fields. The letters themselves are never stored.

How is this different from Screen Studio or Loom?

Booya is free, runs on all three desktop systems, and does its captions and edits on your machine instead of in someone else's cloud. It also blurs secrets and comes with a command line tool, so you can script the whole pipeline.

I found a bug. Where do I report it?

Open an issue on GitHub. Include your OS, the Booya version, and audio.log from the recording's project folder if it is about sound. Security problems go through SECURITY.md instead.
