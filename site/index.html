Record it. Booya.

A free, open source screen recorder for product demos. Auto zoom, smooth cursor, styled backgrounds. No account, no telemetry.

Download Source

🔍 Auto zoom

Zooms on clicks using the event log recorded alongside the video.

🎙 Locked sound

Audio is laid back over the real recording time, so voice and picture stay together, even if a mic drops samples.

🕸 Runs locally

Everything stays on your machine. Cloud features are off until you turn them on.

📦 Any format

Export MP4, WebM or GIF up to 4K in 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1.
