DemoLens 0.7.0 fixes the Windows microphone, makes auto zoom far calmer, fixes missing captions and silent editor playback, and adds device frames, motion blur, music, per clip mute, system audio on Windows and macOS, and a first run permissions screen on a Mac.

## Windows microphone
Voice was missing from Windows recordings. No single cause can be proven without the hardware, but the code had several real defects, all fixed:
- Errors from ffmpeg's DirectShow capture were swallowed under the hidden console window, so nothing said why. The ffmpeg output now goes to `audio.log` in the project folder, and the editor shows "Mic not recorded: reason".
- The level meter in the web view could still hold the microphone when ffmpeg opened it. The meter is now stopped (by the app, not the page) before recording starts.
- The device chosen in the list and the name handed to the recorder could differ (friendly name against alternative name, quoting, non-ASCII names, the default device).
- A silent microphone was never noticed. The UI now warns within 2 seconds when the level stays at zero, and the Windows setting "Let desktop apps access your microphone" gets its own message with a link.
- The final join silently dropped a sound file it could not read.
- There was no in-process path. On Windows the microphone is now recorded through WASAPI (cpal) and written on the same clock as the screen, with DirectShow only as the fallback.
- `demolens doctor --audio` records 3 seconds and reports the level, and a Windows CI test checks that pipeline errors reach the user.

## Auto zoom
- The camera stays on the whole screen and zooms only for a cluster of clicks (3 in one small area, about a fifth of the screen, within 4.5 s on Normal; the count and window are settings), a burst of typing over 1.5 s, or a marker.
- Gone from the defaults: zooms on a resting cursor, on a changed screen or a sliding window, on a single click and cursor following. They made the camera move with no click.
- No zoom in the first second. The whole screen stays up at least 1.5 s between zooms. Slow spring easing (about 0.9 s), long holds, a dead zone for small cursor moves.
- Scrolling is logged on all systems. While scrolling the camera eases out to the full screen and stays there until 0.8 s after it stops.
- A typical minute now gets 3 to 6 zooms at most.

## Captions
Some sentences got no caption. Whisper's small model often gets the words right but not their times: it holds one word over several seconds, then piles the rest into one instant or past the end of the audio. A caption with no time on screen never shows, and words that were heard again in a gap were dropped by their time. Word times are now brought back onto the voice (pile-ups spread out, unbelievable times replaced by a proportional layout over the voiced stretches, words in silence moved onto the voice), a stretch that gave almost nothing is heard once more without whisper's "not speech" shortcut, and a caption stays at least 0.6 s. A regression test uses the pattern of the report's 20 s clip (the middle sentence drifting late, a dozen words stamped at the very end). The exact output of the user's model could not be reproduced here, and the video is not in the repository.

## Editor playback
Editor sound was silent in the macOS app. Playback now runs natively (cpal, with ffmpeg decoding to PCM) on Windows and macOS, in step with the video, with drift correction (re-seek past 60 ms). The web audio element stays as the fallback. Settings > Audio diagnostics shows what the web view reports and writes it to the log.

## New
- **Device frames**: laptop, browser and phone (simple vector shapes).
- **Motion blur** on zoom and pan moves (off by default).
- **Music**: import a file, volume, ducking under the voice, fade in and out, start point and loop.
- **Mute a clip**, and **camera pins** at the four corners and the middle of each edge.
- **System audio** on Windows (WASAPI loopback) and macOS (ScreenCaptureKit, macOS 13 or newer), off by default, recorded on the side and mixed into the voice track. Nothing playing is fine and leaves no file.
- **First run permissions** on a Mac: Screen Recording (required), Microphone, Input Monitoring and System audio, with a button for each that asks or opens the exact Settings page, green checks that update every second, and a notice when an update switched one off. Also under Settings > Permissions. Windows and Linux show the microphone only. DemoLens never sends your recordings anywhere.

## Honest limits
- The Windows and macOS capture, WASAPI and ScreenCaptureKit audio, native playback and the permission calls were verified by CI compile and unit tests only. They have not been run on a real Windows PC or a Mac.
- The web view of the macOS app (WKWebView) was not available for testing.
- Native playback shifts pitch at speeds other than 1x. The web audio element keeps pitch.
- Wayland is still not supported, and the earlier limits in 0.5.2 still apply.
